Quick Rules Review

Keep these two golden rules in mind while practicing.

1. Separability (Phrasal Verbs)

Some phrasal verbs can be separated by their object (especially pronouns like it). Others cannot.

  • ✅ Call it off (Separable)
  • ❌ Call off it
  • ✅ Look forward to it (Inseparable)

2. Figurative Meaning (Idioms)

Idioms never mean what they literally say. You must understand the cultural or figurative context.

  • "Hit the sack" = Go to sleep
  • (Not physically punching a sack)
