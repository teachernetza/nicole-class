Present: Simple vs Continuous ⚖️

Habits & Routines 🔄 vs. Actions happening right now ⚡

Present Simple 📊

Facts, schedules, and routines.
Chunks: Do you / Does she + Verb base

"They usually analyze data 📉"

"What do they analyze? 🤔"

Present Continuous 🏃

Right now or temporary situations.
Chunks: Are you / Is he + Verb-ing

"We are evaluating the results 📋"

"Are you evaluating them too? 🧐"

⏪

Past: Simple vs Continuous ⚖️

Finished actions 🛑 vs. Actions in progress in the past 🎞️

Past Simple 🕰️

Completed events at a specific time.
Chunks: Did you + Verb base

"She delegated the tasks 👥"

"Did you delegate yours? 📝"

Past Continuous 🌊

Background actions or interrupted events.
Chunks: Were you / Was she + Verb-ing

"I was troubleshooting 🔧"

"What were you doing? 🕵️"

🏆

Present Perfect 🌟

Life experiences & past actions connected to NOW. 🔗

Structure: Have/Has + Past Participle (e.g., implemented, analyzed, seen)

Experiences 🌍

Have you ever implemented a new system? ⚙️

Recent Results 🎯

They have already assembled the team. 🏗️

🔮

Future Forms 🚀

Will (Decisions/Promises) vs Going to (Plans/Intentions). 🗓️

Will 💡

Instant decisions, promises, and general predictions.

  • I will negotiate the contract 🤝
  • When will they evaluate us? 📋

Going to 📅

Established plans and clear intentions.

  • We are going to brainstorm 🧠
  • What are you going to implement? ⚙️
