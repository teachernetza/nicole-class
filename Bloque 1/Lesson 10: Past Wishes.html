How do we talk about things we want to change? Whether it's a current dissatisfaction, a past regret, or an annoying habit, these structures are your best tools.

2. The Core Concept

The Golden Rule of "Unreal" Time.

When we use "I wish" or "If only," we are talking about situations that are not real or not true right now. Because they are imaginary, we must step one tense back in time.

Want to change the Present?

Use Past Simple

Want to change the Past?

Use Past Perfect

Want to change a Behavior?

Use Would + Verb

3. Present Desires

I wish + Past Simple

I wish + Subject + Past Simple

Reality: I don't have a car.

I wish I had a car.

Reality: I can't speak French.

I wish I could speak French.

Grammar Note for Verb 'To Be'

In formal English, we use WERE for all subjects (I, he, she, it).
Reality: I am not rich. ➔ I wish I WERE rich.

5. Past Regrets

I wish + Past Perfect

I wish + Subject + Past Perfect (had + V3)

Reality (Past): I ate too much pizza last night.

I wish I hadn't eaten so much pizza.

Reality (Past): I didn't study for the exam.

I wish I had studied for the exam.

7. Annoyances & Complaints

I wish + Would

I wish + Subject + WOULD + Base Verb

We use this when someone's behavior (or the weather) annoys us, and we want it to change.

Reality: You are tapping your pen on the desk. It's annoying.

I wish you would stop tapping your pen.

Reality: It is raining, and I want to go out.

I wish it would stop raining.

Grammar Note

You cannot use "would" for yourself (I wish I would). You can only use it for other people or things!

9. "If Only..."

Adding Drama and Emotion

"If only" follows the exact same grammar rules as "I wish", but it is much stronger, more emphatic, and more emotional.

Standard Wish

I wish I knew the answer.

(A simple desire)

Dramatic Wish

If only I knew the answer!

(Desperation! Frustration!)

10. Vocabulary Building

Words to express feelings

Regret (noun/verb)

To feel sad about a mistake you made in the past.
"I regret not studying."

Dissatisfied (adj)

Not happy or pleased with a current situation.
"He is dissatisfied with his job."

Annoying (adj)

Causing irritation.
"That loud noise is really annoying."

12. Reading Comprehension

The Tale of Timmy's Regrets

A Very Bad Day

Timmy sat on the side of the highway, staring at smoke coming from his car engine. He felt deeply dissatisfied with his recent choices. Last week, he had a choice between buying a reliable Honda and a cheap, rusty sports car. He chose the sports car.

"If only I had listened to my dad," he muttered to himself. "He told me not to buy it. I wish I had bought the Honda."

To make matters worse, it started to rain. Timmy didn't have an umbrella. "I wish it would stop raining," he yelled at the sky. A truck drove by, splashing mud all over his new shoes. Timmy sighed. "I wish I were at home in my warm bed right now." It was a day full of regrets.
